# Onboarding Handbook

Reference HR-2024-11.

## First Day

New joiners collect their laptop from the IT desk between 09:00 and 11:00. Accounts are created
in advance and credentials are handed over in person after identity verification.

## First Week

Complete the information security training module. This is mandatory and must be finished within
five working days of starting. Managers receive a report of outstanding completions weekly.

## Probation

The standard probation period is six months. A probation review is held at three months and again
at six. Probation may be extended once by up to three months with written notice.

## Buddy Scheme

Every new joiner is assigned a buddy from a different team for the first month. The buddy scheme
is coordinated by human resources.
