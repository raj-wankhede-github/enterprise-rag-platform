# Leave and Absence Policy

Effective 1 March 2025. Reference HR-2025-03.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, accruing monthly. Up to
five unused days may be carried into the following year and must be taken before 31 March.

## Parental Leave

Employees with twelve months of continuous service are entitled to 18 weeks of paid parental
leave at full salary. A further 8 weeks may be taken unpaid. Parental leave must be requested at
least 8 weeks before the intended start date using form HR-14.

Parental leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first two years following
birth or adoption.

## Sick Leave

Employees may take up to 10 days of sick leave per year without a medical certificate. Absences
beyond three consecutive days require a certificate from a registered practitioner.

## Unpaid Leave

Requests for unpaid leave longer than four weeks require approval from both the line manager and
the head of human resources.
