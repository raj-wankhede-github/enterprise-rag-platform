# Expenses: Frequently Asked Questions

Reference FIN-2025-06. This document summarises common questions. Where it differs from the
Travel and Expense Policy, the policy takes precedence.

## How long do I have to submit a claim?

Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 60
days require written approval from the head of finance.

## Can I claim for a taxi?

Taxis may be claimed where public transport is impractical, unsafe, or where luggage makes it
unreasonable. Journeys under two kilometres are not normally reimbursed.

## What about mileage in my own car?

Mileage is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre. A journey log showing date, route and purpose
must accompany the claim.

## Who approves my claim?

Claims are approved by your line manager. Claims over 1,000 EUR are additionally reviewed by
finance before payment.
