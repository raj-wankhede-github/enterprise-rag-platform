# Meeting Room Booking

Reference FAC-2024-09.

## Booking

Rooms are booked through the calendar system. Recurring bookings longer than eight weeks require
facilities approval.

## Release Rule

A room not occupied within ten minutes of the booking start is released automatically and may be
taken by anyone.

## Large Rooms

The two large rooms on the third floor may not be booked for fewer than six attendees between
09:00 and 17:00.
