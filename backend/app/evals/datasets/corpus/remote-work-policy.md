# Remote Work Policy

Effective 1 April 2025. Reference HR-2025-04.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed probation may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote
arrangements require approval from the department head and a written variation to the employment
contract.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and one external monitor. A contribution of up to 400 EUR towards
a desk and chair is available once every three years, claimed through the expense portal under
category HOME-SETUP.

## Core Hours

Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 16:00 in their contracted time zone.
Meetings outside core hours require agreement in advance.

## Working From Another Country

Working from a country other than the contracted one is permitted for up to 20 working days per
calendar year and must be notified to human resources in advance. Longer periods create tax
obligations and require approval from both human resources and finance.
