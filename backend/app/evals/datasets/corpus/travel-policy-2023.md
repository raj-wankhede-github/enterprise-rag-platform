# Travel and Expense Policy (2023)

Effective 1 January 2023. Superseded by TE-2025-01 on 1 January 2025. Reference TE-2023-04.

## Per Diem Allowance

Employees travelling on company business may claim a per diem allowance without receipts.

| Destination grade | Per diem |
| --- | --- |
| A | 100 EUR |
| B | 75 EUR |
| C | 55 EUR |

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single item over 35 EUR.

## Booking

All flights must be booked in economy class regardless of duration. Exceptions require written
approval from the chief financial officer.
