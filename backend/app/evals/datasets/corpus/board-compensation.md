# Board Compensation Framework

Reference BRD-2025-01. Restricted: not published to staff.

## Executive Pay Bands

The zarquon executive compensation band is set annually by the remuneration committee. Band
boundaries are reviewed against an external benchmark in September each year.

## Long-Term Incentive

Awards vest over four years with a one-year cliff. Vesting is contingent on continued service and
on the company meeting the performance conditions set at grant.

## Non-Executive Fees

Non-executive directors receive a fixed annual fee. Committee chairs receive an additional fee.
Fees are reviewed every two years.
