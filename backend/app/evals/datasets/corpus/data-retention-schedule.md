# Data Retention Schedule

Effective 1 January 2025. Reference DR-2025-01.

## Retention Periods

| Record type | Retention period | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee personnel files | 7 years after leaving | Legal obligation |
| Job applications (unsuccessful) | 6 months | Legitimate interest |
| Payroll records | 10 years | Tax law |
| Expense claims and receipts | 7 years | Tax law |
| Customer support tickets | 3 years | Legitimate interest |
| Security event logs | 12 months | Legitimate interest |
| CCTV footage | 30 days | Legitimate interest |
| Marketing consent records | Until withdrawn plus 2 years | Consent |

## Deletion

Records reaching the end of their retention period are deleted in the quarterly deletion run.
Deletion is irreversible and is logged.

## Legal Hold

Where records are subject to a legal hold, retention periods are suspended until the hold is
lifted by the legal team. A legal hold overrides every period in the table above.
