# Information Security Policy

Effective 1 February 2025. Reference SEC-2025-02.

## Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all
accounts with access to production systems. Passwords may not be reused across systems and are
not required to be rotated on a schedule.

## Device Encryption

All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before being issued. The IT team verifies
encryption status during onboarding and quarterly thereafter.

## Section 4.2 - Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident must be reported to the security team within one hour of
discovery. Reports go to the security channel or to security@ by email. Do not investigate a
suspected compromise yourself.

Incident severity is classified as follows.

| Severity | Definition | Response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Confirmed data exposure | 15 minutes |
| S2 | Suspected compromise, no confirmed exposure | 1 hour |
| S3 | Policy violation with no compromise | 1 business day |

## Third-Party Access

Vendor access to production requires a signed data processing agreement and is granted for a
maximum of 90 days before review.
