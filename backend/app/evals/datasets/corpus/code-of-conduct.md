# Code of Conduct

Reference HR-2024-01.

## Expected Behaviour

Treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect. Disagree with ideas rather than with
people. Assume good intent while still naming impact.

## Conflicts of Interest

Declare any outside employment, directorship or financial interest in a supplier or competitor to
human resources within fourteen days of it arising. Declaration does not imply prohibition; most
declared interests are simply recorded.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Gifts with a value over 75 EUR may not be accepted. Hospitality may be accepted where it is
proportionate and declared if it exceeds 150 EUR in value.

## Raising Concerns

Concerns may be raised with a line manager, with human resources, or anonymously through the
whistleblowing line. Retaliation against anyone raising a concern in good faith is itself a
disciplinary matter.
