# Travel and Expense Policy

Effective 1 January 2025. Supersedes the 2023 Travel and Expense Policy. Reference TE-2025-01.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors travelling on company business. It does not
cover relocation, which is governed by the Relocation Policy.

## Per Diem Allowance

Employees travelling on company business may claim a per diem allowance. The allowance is paid
without receipts and depends on the destination grade.

| Destination grade | Per diem | Overnight cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A (London, Zurich, Tokyo) | 120 EUR | 260 EUR |
| B (Berlin, Madrid, Warsaw) | 90 EUR | 180 EUR |
| C (all other locations) | 65 EUR | 130 EUR |

Grade A applies to the cities listed and to any destination the finance team designates in
writing before travel begins.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single item over 50 EUR. Items below that threshold may be claimed
on the expense line without documentation. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any grade.

## Booking

Flights over four hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires written
approval from a department head before booking. All travel must be booked through the corporate
travel desk under reference TE-2025-01.

## Escalation

If a claim is rejected the employee may escalate to their line manager within ten working days of
the rejection notice. The line manager consults the finance business partner, who reviews the
original submission and the rejection note. Where the two disagree the matter passes to the head
of finance, whose decision closes the escalation. Ticket TKT-99812 tracks exceptions to this
procedure.
