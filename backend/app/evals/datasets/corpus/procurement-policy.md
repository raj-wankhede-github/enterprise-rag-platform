# Procurement Policy

Effective 1 January 2025. Reference PROC-2025-01.

## Approval Thresholds

| Purchase value | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Under 2,000 EUR | Line manager |
| 2,000 to 25,000 EUR | Department head |
| 25,000 to 100,000 EUR | Chief financial officer |
| Over 100,000 EUR | Board |

## Preferred Suppliers

Purchases of standard office equipment must be made through a preferred supplier unless no
preferred supplier can meet the requirement. Current preferred supplier codes are SUP-4471
(IT hardware), SUP-2280 (office furniture) and SUP-9013 (print and stationery).

## Purchase Orders

Every purchase over 2,000 EUR requires a purchase order raised before the commitment is made.
Retrospective purchase orders are not permitted and invoices without a matching order will be
returned unpaid.

## Contract Review

Any contract with an initial term over twelve months must be reviewed by legal before signature.
